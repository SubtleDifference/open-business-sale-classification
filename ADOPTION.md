# Adoption

Open Business Sale Classification is voluntary; no adopters are recorded yet. Listing here is optional and is not required to use the catalogue.

Providers may adopt shared IDs directly or map their own categories and codes. Their terminology, browsing structures and feed formats can stay unchanged. Any mappings are maintained by the implementing sender or receiver and may remain private; publication is not required.

Business Characteristics are optional. Providers may support all, some or none without making them industry activities. An unsupported characteristic can be ignored while an otherwise valid activity classification remains valid.

Adoption does not establish a working integration. Sender and receiver must agree on the format, catalogue version and classification behaviour; see the [interoperability guide](docs/integration.md).

With the provider's agreement, record its name and role, catalogue version, direct use or mapping approach, characteristic support and material limitations. Include public documentation or provider confirmation and the date checked. Private implementation details are not required.

Use the same criteria and alphabetical presentation for every provider, with no paid or preferred placement. Interest alone does not establish adoption; a listing is not certification or endorsement.
