# Draft review questions

Open Business Sale Classification `0.5.0-draft.1` contains 196 activities, nine optional characteristics and 20 browsing groups. Activity and characteristic limits are independent.

## Focused taxonomy questions

These are questions for stakeholder review, not implemented taxonomy changes. Assess each against the [category-necessity and buyer-discovery criteria](../CONTRIBUTING.md#category-proposals); a split or new activity needs evidence that existing activities, combinations, aliases and characteristics are insufficient.

| Area | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Bakery c0051 | The description emphasises an own retail outlet. Should production-only or wholesale bakeries use this activity or Food and Beverage Manufacturing? |
| Health Technology c0205 | This healthcare-specific activity overlaps software, equipment and technical services. Confirm when it adds useful activity detail rather than a redundant sector label. |
| EV Charging c0208 and Energy Storage c0236 | Both span several commercial roles. Check that operation, installation and equipment supply boundaries remain clear beside Electrical and Renewable Energy Services. |
| Bicycle and Motorcycle Businesses c0017 | Confirm the combined product/service scope is useful to adopters before considering any split. |
| Civil c0078 and Electrical c0165 | The definitions are clearer than the short labels. Consider a label clarification if stakeholders find the names ambiguous, keeping IDs where meaning is unchanged. |


## Before v1.0

Bakery's production/retail boundary and Health Technology's role alongside other activities need explicit scope decisions before v1.0. The grouping and short-label questions are feedback items, not automatic release blockers where definitions remain usable.

Editorial statuses currently distinguish 131 active-labelled activities, 65 proposed activities and nine proposed characteristics. All are part of this draft; these labels imply neither adoption nor endorsement. Proposed status alone does not determine suitability for a stable release.

Resolve or explicitly document material scope decisions, confirm integration examples and selection rules, then freeze the release metadata. See the [release checklist](../ROADMAP.md).

Automated checks validate catalogue structure and classification behaviour. They do not establish receiver acceptance, stakeholder agreement or independent domain validation.
